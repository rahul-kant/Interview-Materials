# FAANG Interview Overview for Senior Java Tech Lead (14 YOE)

## Current FAANG Interview Pattern (2025)

### Interview Rounds Structure

For a **Senior/Staff Software Engineer** or **Tech Lead** position with 14 years of experience, expect the following rounds:

#### 1. **Phone/Virtual Screen (1-2 rounds)**
- Coding + Problem Solving (45-60 mins)
- Focus: Medium to Hard LeetCode-style problems
- May include preliminary system design discussion

#### 2. **Onsite/Virtual Onsite (4-6 rounds)**

**Round A: Coding Interviews (2-3 rounds)**
- Each round: 45-60 minutes
- Difficulty: Medium to Hard problems
- Expected: Optimal solutions with time/space complexity analysis
- Live coding with explanation of thought process

**Round B: System Design (1-2 rounds)**
- Each round: 45-60 minutes
- Design scalable distributed systems
- For senior roles: Expected to lead the discussion
- Deep dive into trade-offs, bottlenecks, and optimization

**Round C: Behavioral/Leadership (1 round)**
- 45-60 minutes
- Amazon: LP (Leadership Principles) based
- Google: Googleyness & Leadership
- Focus on past experiences, conflict resolution, team leadership

**Round D: Domain Expertise (Optional, 1 round)**
- Java/Backend deep dive
- Concurrency, JVM internals, performance optimization
- Architecture and design patterns

## Company-Specific Patterns

### **Amazon**
- **Rounds**: 5-6 (1 coding screen + 4-5 onsite)
- **Focus**: 
  - 2 Coding rounds (LeetCode Medium/Hard)
  - 1 System Design
  - 2 Leadership Principles (LP) behavioral
- **Key**: Strong emphasis on LPs (14 principles)
- **Bar Raiser**: One interviewer is a "Bar Raiser" - extra tough

### **Google**
- **Rounds**: 5-6 (1-2 phone + 4-5 onsite)
- **Focus**:
  - 2-3 Coding rounds (Algorithm-heavy, CS fundamentals)
  - 1 System Design
  - 1 Googleyness & Leadership
- **Key**: Very algorithm-focused, clean code, optimal solutions
- **Unique**: Team matching happens after clearing interviews

### **Meta (Facebook)**
- **Rounds**: 5-6 (1-2 phone + 4-5 onsite)
- **Focus**:
  - 2 Coding rounds
  - 1-2 System Design (product-focused)
  - 1 Behavioral
- **Key**: Move fast, product thinking, scalability
- **Unique**: Strong focus on building products used by billions

### **Apple**
- **Rounds**: 5-7 (1-2 phone + 4-6 onsite)
- **Focus**:
  - 2 Coding rounds
  - 1 System Design
  - 1-2 Domain expertise (deep technical)
  - 1 Behavioral
- **Key**: Product quality, attention to detail, long-term thinking

### **Microsoft**
- **Rounds**: 4-5 (1 phone + 3-4 onsite)
- **Focus**:
  - 2 Coding rounds
  - 1 System Design
  - 1 Behavioral
- **Key**: Practical problem-solving, collaboration
- **As-Appropriate**: Final round with senior leader

### **Netflix**
- **Rounds**: 4-6 rounds
- **Focus**:
  - Very high bar (senior engineers only)
  - Strong system design
  - Cultural fit (Freedom & Responsibility)
- **Key**: Top 10% talent, high compensation

## Success Metrics

### **Coding Interviews**
- Solve 2-3 problems in 45-60 minutes
- Write clean, bug-free code
- Explain time/space complexity
- Handle edge cases
- Optimize solutions

### **System Design**
- Drive the discussion (don't wait for prompts)
- Start with requirements gathering
- Propose high-level architecture
- Deep dive into components
- Discuss trade-offs and bottlenecks
- Scale from 1M to 1B users

### **Behavioral**
- Use STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Show leadership, impact, and learning
- Be specific with metrics
- Demonstrate growth mindset
