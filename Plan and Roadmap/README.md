## 🔥 Top Tips for Success

### 1. **Consistency Over Intensity**
- Daily 2-3 hours > Weekend binge studying
- Build habit, avoid burnout

### 2. **Quality Over Quantity**
- Understand patterns, don't memorize solutions
- 400 understood problems > 1000 memorized solutions

### 3. **Mock Interviews Are Critical**
- 20+ mocks = 2-3x higher pass rate
- Start early (Week 2), not Week 15!

### 4. **Behavioral = 30-40% of Success**
- Don't skip for senior roles
- Amazon: 40% of interview is behavioral!

### 5. **Company-Specific Preparation**
- Each FAANG is different
- Tailor prep based on target company

### 6. **Take Care of Yourself**
- Sleep 8 hours
- Exercise regularly
- Manage stress
- Marathon, not sprint

## 📞 When You're Ready

### Application Strategy:
1. **Month 4**: Start applying
2. **Apply to 3-5 companies**: Don't put all eggs in one basket
3. **Sequence wisely**: 
   - Apply to "backup" companies first (practice)
   - Save top choice for last
4. **Get referrals**: LinkedIn connections, 2-3x better chance
5. **Space interviews**: 1-2 weeks apart

### Interview Timeline:
```
Week 1: Apply with referrals
Week 2-3: Recruiter calls, schedule interviews
Week 4-5: Phone screens
Week 6-8: Onsite interviews
Week 9-10: Offers and negotiation
```
